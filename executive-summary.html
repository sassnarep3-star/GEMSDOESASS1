Why GEMSDOE32’s 0.2778 is the group best — and why copying it cannot beat it

  1. Bytes (MEASURED-HERE, previous session): h33-h33-2-b2 is exactly dotted-h19-5-d2-8 (44,090 dots, live 0.2600) minus the 6,436 dots within 2 px of the published catalogue = 37,654 dots, pixel-identical (sym-diff 0).
  2. Metric (official DTI α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangle; known-fault pixels ignored): emitted mass is taxed at 0.2; a dot 1–2 px off a mapped scarp is pure FP. Deleting those dots raised 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778. That is emission efficiency, not a new geological field.
  3. Habitat: a catalogue-flank file (h66, median 5.8 px) scored 0.0742 live; the champion’s median is 19.6 px (~2 km). Hidden labels are far-field lidar scarps.
  4. Can we beat 0.2778? Only with a better field at ~37–44 k dots that also clears the lattice rule (or a versioned policy amendment). H-PBSC is a different field and is unique vs the 17 competitive rasters, but it lost holdout (0.0650 vs 0.1011) and is 99.48% within 3 px of the calibration lattice.
