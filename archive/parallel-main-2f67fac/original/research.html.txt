<main id="main" class="shell"><div class="page-hero"><div class="breadcrumb"><a href="index.html">Overview</a> &nbsp;/&nbsp; Research</div><h1>Better questions, before better maps.</h1><p>Four geological hypotheses for a future assigned lane. Their ranking is qualitative—not a predicted leaderboard score. None has been tested in this checkout.</p></div>
<div class="page-grid"><div><section class="panel page-card"><div class="section-kicker">Prospective hypothesis slate</div><h2>Start with independent physical evidence</h2><p>The competition scores fault locations, not geothermal vents directly. These ideas may help identify structures relevant to geothermal systems, but each has non-fault explanations. No lane was specified, so no model or holdout experiment was run.</p>
<div class="hypothesis"><div class="pill-label">01 · Higher potential / higher cost · untested</div><h3>Cross-flight-line magnetic consistency</h3><p><strong>Layers &amp; transform:</strong> GeoDAWN magnetic flight/tie-line profiles; compare aligned step and gradient residuals across independent lines, with optional gravity corroboration. A buried break could repeat beneath covered terrain, including where the catalogue has no trace.</p><p><strong>Could instead be:</strong> lithologic contacts, dike swarms, flight-line levelling or striping. This differs from filtering a gridded raster; a related site proposed the idea but did not report implementing it. Source archives, coverage and processing remain unverified locally.</p><a class="source" href="https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and">Official USGS GeoDAWN release ↗</a></div>
<div class="hypothesis"><div class="pill-label">02 · Medium potential / medium–high cost · untested</div><h3>Lithology-conditioned radiometric dipoles</h3><p><strong>Layers &amp; transform:</strong> potassium, thorium and uranium ratios; residualize by lithology and compare opposing sides of a candidate axis, with an independent potential-field edge. A hidden, permeable structure could host asymmetric alteration without a visible scarp.</p><p><strong>Could instead be:</strong> weathering, moisture, sorting or bedrock contacts. Related work already uses radiometry, so global novelty is not established. Appropriate aligned data and coverage still need checking.</p><a class="source" href="https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/657e1d85d34e23d3533209f7">Official USGS data catalog ↗</a></div>
<div class="hypothesis"><div class="pill-label">03 · Medium potential / high cost · untested</div><h3>Paired thermal and geochemical gradients</h3><p><strong>Layers &amp; transform:</strong> GDR well/spring chemistry and temperature plus shallow probe data; compare nearby samples with uncertainty and spatial validation, rather than simply measuring distance to a hot point. Contrasts may outline concealed fluid corridors.</p><p><strong>Could instead be:</strong> groundwater mixing, seasons, drilling and biased sampling. Thermal point features have been tried elsewhere; exclusivity, sample coverage and shareability are not verified.</p><a class="source" href="https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391">DOE OpenEI INGENIOUS data ↗</a></div>
<div class="hypothesis"><div class="pill-label">04 · Uncertain potential / high cost · untested</div><h3>Multiscale drainage displacement</h3><p><strong>Layers &amp; transform:</strong> 3DEP elevation and drainage paths; look for consistent upstream/downstream offsets across a candidate lineament, corroborated by transverse curvature at several scales. A young structure can offset channels where individual scarps are eroded.</p><p><strong>Could instead be:</strong> fan avulsion, landslides, erosion, human channels or lithology. Terrain features exist in previous work; uniqueness and local tile coverage are unverified.</p><a class="source" href="https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program">Official USGS 3DEP ↗</a></div>
<p class="note">Each idea is currently <strong>not yet viable</strong>: this run did not obtain and validate its external inputs. No cross-site novelty claim is made. <a href="knowledge/research-preregistration.md">Read full slate, evidence and gate protocol →</a></p></section></div>
<aside><section class="panel page-card"><div class="section-kicker">Research discipline</div><h2>Run only after preflight</h2><ul><li>One explicit method lane, complete registry, agreed shared template and reusable cache.</li><li>Feature-alone leakage canaries and pre-placement surface comparison.</li><li>Whole-segment buffered holdout; visible-only catalogue features; exact visible-pixel mask.</li><li>Pooled DTI and paired spatial 95% CI against a matched best.</li><li>Final-dot uniqueness, exact-file format and separate slot selector.</li></ul><a class="text-link" href="knowledge/next-session.md">Read the next-session plan →</a></section><section class="panel page-card"><div class="section-kicker">Scientific context</div><h2>Use multiple lines of evidence</h2><p>The USGS Great Basin play-fairway synthesis documents blind geothermal examples supported by geological and geophysical evidence. This supports integrated reasoning, not an estimated score for any hypothesis above.</p><a class="text-link" href="https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and">Read the USGS synthesis ↗</a></section></aside></div></main>
