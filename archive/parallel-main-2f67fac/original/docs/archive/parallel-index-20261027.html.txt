<h2>PhD-Level Forensic Autopsy — Why 0.2778 Leads (and why it may not hold)</h2>
<p><b>What H33-2-B2 actually is:</b> start from the 0.2708 base (<code>GEMSDOE31 h27-4-solo-d28 40,199 dots</code>, itself a clean 2026-10-04 rebuild of the H19-5 power-law budgeted multiline corroborated surface — 121k→0.1922, 60k d1.5→0.2477, 44k d2.8→0.2600, with Poisson 300 m emission and submodular max-cover). <b>H33-2-B2 deletes every dot with d(catalogue) ≤2 px (200 m)</b>: 2,545 dots removed (40,199→37,654), 0 within 100 m and 0 within 200 m of any mapped fault, min NN 2.83 px retained. The owner ledger reports live 0.2778 for that pruned raster; the unpruned 40,199-dots ledger is 0.2708; the 35,483-dots B3 prune drops to 0.264. In short, <b>the win is a flank prune, not a new detector</b>.</p>

<details><summary>Mechanistic explanation — why pruning helps live but hurts the proxy</summary>
<p><b>Metric asymmetry matters.</b> Live DTI uses α 0.2 (FP) and β 0.8 (FN) with a 300 m triangular kernel: false positives near truth are discounted (1 − d/300), false negatives are punished 4× harder than false positives. A dot <i>on</i> a mapped fault is invisible to the live scorer (truth is only new faults), but in the <i>catalogue-hidden proxy</i> that omits visible faults from the domain (`active = valid & ~known`), a dot on a mapped fault is <i>free</i> (never charged α·FP). B2 therefore looks <i>worse</i> on the historical proxy (catalogue_hidden 0.0046 vs 0.098 for D2.8) yet <i>better</i> live — precisely the documented instrument defect (Hedge-v2 0.281→0.225 when strict). The 2-px flank is the sweet spot: it discards the free-but-live-expensive collar while retaining the 6.5× concentration (37,654 dots at 0.0592 blind coverage) that still captures tip/step-over corridors.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>What the 9-site clone cluster tells us</summary>
<p>All nine 0.2449–0.2778 historic rasters sit in one H19-5 multiline family. Pairwise 3-px overlaps are 86.7–100% (72 comparisons). Their emission budgets are the only monotone ranker (more dots → lower live, but higher proxy). Across families the proxy-live Spearman is ~0.51 (p≈0.09, n=12) — drift, not a predictor. That is why H33-A…D label-free surfaces (Hessian orientation consensus, valley chains, scarplet chains, MT straight boundaries) all failed the same-instrument holdout (0.018–0.033 vs 0.148 control) yet the flank prune succeeded: <b>geometry, not physics, moved the needle at this dot budget</b>. The next physics win needs to beat B2 <i>without</i> re-adding a flank.</p>
</details>
