The decision

This run is negative: zero model experiments and zero competition submissions. Four prospective geological ideas were ranked, but the designated single-method paragraph was absent. The shared prepared feature stack and a complete current registry were not available. The shared template also has open evaluator, writer and release-gate issues that require upstream fixes. A separate selector must decide whether a future qualifying result may use a slot.

For the usual 37,654-point budget, one pinned registry lattice alone forces at least 82.34% of points within three pixels, above the stipulated 70% duplicate stop. Five later peer candidates and the separate synthetic raster already in this repository independently tested at 99.17–100% against that same lattice; promotional banners do not override the literal stop. This is not a finding that every smaller output is necessarily a duplicate.

The user-reported 0.2778 for GEMSDOE32 is not backed by a receipt in the inspected evidence. Thinning and catalogue-near pruning are plausible reasons for a better score on the published metric, but their causal contribution and generalization are unknown. We cannot estimate whether an unrun unique method will beat it.

How to submit, if a future run passes

These are conditional instructions, not authorization to upload today.

01 Produce a distinct, tested candidate

Run only a designated method, with held-out whole fault segments and a buffer. Derive catalogue-dependent features from visible faults only. Compare each feature for suspicious AUC above 0.90 and report HOLDOUT-DTI, the evaluator version, withheld count, and 95% CI against a fair baseline. Check rank correlation of the pre-placement surface against the complete current registry, and the final dots for the specified three-pixel duplicate rule. Stop on a breach.

02 Validate the actual saved TIFF

Use the official, authenticated sample grid and the shared submission writer after its continuous-probability issue is fixed upstream. Independently reopen the exact bytes: one float32 band, matching CRS, shape and transform, finite values in [0, 1] within the footprint, no interior NaNs, and correct null/NaN outside. Our local checker also checks against the observed mirror grid (not organizer-authenticated) and requires finite raw storage everywhere and an embedded mask outside; it is conservative and is not organizer acceptance.

A zero-filled raw array with an internal mask is one possible local interpretation, not a promise that the portal accepts it. See the official format instructions. If the portal rejects values as out of range, inspect the saved data, nodata/mask and a true organizer-provided template; do not assume display values or hidden NaNs are safe.

03 Select separately, then upload

Only after the uniqueness, holdout and saved-file checks pass, and a separate selector authorizes a slot, sign in to the official competition. Check the actual allowance on the site; staff say the website limit governs and the window rolls. Upload the selected GeoTIFF in the competition submission interface with its unique name and a truthful note of at most 140 characters. Preserve the file SHA-256 and submission receipt. Follow applicable rules, including generative-AI disclosure.

Questions that matter

Is the public leaderboard a candidate score?

No. The official public leaderboard was observed at 0.4544 on 27 October 2026. That is neither a private result nor a receipt for any file reviewed here, and may change. Conflicting user-reported values of 0.4413 and 0.3195 were not verified as the current leader.

Why not use one of the “OK to submit” files?

For five pinned peer candidates and the separate synthetic TIFF already in this repository, we measured over 99% of their dots within three pixels of a registered lattice. That breaches this run's literal stop rule, even if a narrower peer comparison or an older banner says otherwise. See the actual-byte audit.

Can the local validator guarantee acceptance?

No. It checks a conservative interpretation of the published raster contract, including raw pixel values and an internal outside-footprint mask. An authenticated template, all organizer processing, accepted receipt, complete registry and actual scoring are outside its scope.
