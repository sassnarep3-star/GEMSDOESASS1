02 / WHAT THE EVIDENCE SAYS

Why the reported leader may have worked.

The evidence supports an explanation, not a causal finding. Private test labels and submission receipts remain unavailable.

BYTE FORENSICS

One family, not nine independent wins.

The GEMSDOE25 and 30 masks are identical in the measured footprint. The GEMSDOE32 B2 mask equals that family after removing pixels within two pixels of the visible catalogue. GEMSDOE36 adds 1,200 pixels to B2. These are raster facts, not proof of which bytes reached the portal.

Open the audit ↗
METRIC INTERPRETATION

Sparser can be more efficient, not more geological.

For the official distance-weighted Tversky metric, nearby predictions can compete for the same truth coverage while still contributing false-positive mass. Thinning and pruning could improve that tradeoff, but can also remove real missing faults. A better public result is possible, not established here.

03 / A MANDATORY STOP

The novelty rule has a hidden constraint.

A registered, space-filling reference covers nearly the entire mirrored footprint within three pixels. At the commonly used 37,654-dot budget, at least 31,005 candidate dots must fall in that neighborhood—regardless of placement.

The required stop is more than 70%. This proof applies to that budget, not every possible smaller output; avoiding the reference is not, by itself, scientific discovery.
