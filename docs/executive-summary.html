Mechanism audit

What changed in H33-B2?

The actual GEMSDOE32 raster is exactly the site-31 base with 2,545 dots removed within 2 pixels of known faults. It adds no dots. This transformation is byte/array-audited; its reported leaderboard benefit is not independently confirmed.

Ranked hypotheses (untested)

H1–H5

  1. H1 thermal-anchor emission prior (validated on holdout, then lattice-blocked): H-TVGB thermal-anchored field reached HOLDOUT-DTI 0.1287 [0.1231–0.1355] vs bar 0.1011 [0.0956, 0.1072], but full-registry lattice reading is 99.79% — blocked under the literal 70⁡/3⁡px rule.
  2. H2 curvature-tilt density: fault-density from lidar-derived curvature/tilt; expected low marginal gain, cheap to test.
  3. H3 radiometric-halo edge: GeoDAWN radiometric gradient halo around scarps; untested.
  4. H4 seismicity-dilation strain: strain from dilated seismicity; untested.
  5. H5 step-over/splay annulus (H-STEP-OVER): uncatalogued faults 300–900 m from catalogue; generated as a gate-passed artifact but holdout-unevaluated.

Projections are never scores. A holdout run clears the 0.1011 bar only if the segment-withheld hide/recover DTI exceeds it with a 95% CI above the bar.
