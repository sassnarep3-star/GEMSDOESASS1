The instrument is biased, and that is the finding

The champion field has exactly zero pixels in common with the public catalogue (minimum distance 1.0 px) — it was built to exclude mapped faults. Our holdout’s truth is the catalogue. The holdout therefore penalises the field that wins live and rewards fields that predict faults already mapped.

Until a truth set of genuinely unmapped faults exists, a holdout improvement can be an improvement towards the wrong target.
