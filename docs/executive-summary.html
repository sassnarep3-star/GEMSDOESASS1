What remains a hypothesis

Removing predictions with little credit for hidden new faults could reduce weighted false-positive mass while retaining recall. But nearness to an existing fault does not make a dot automatically false: a new nearby splay could earn credit.

Without hidden truth or a submission-page receipt, the reported 0.2778 is not a verified causal result, and it does not calibrate a forecast for a new method.

Can we exceed the reported 0.2778? This session's answer: only with a better field at ~40k dots.
