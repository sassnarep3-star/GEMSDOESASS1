Geological hypotheses (unedited research ideas)

3–5 candidate hypotheses, ranked by expected DTI gain and implementation cost

These are untested research ideas. Validate the top candidate on the spatially-blocked holdout before spending any weekly submission slot.

H1 — Catalogue step-over/splay annulus (THIS SUBMISSION)

Layers: existing_faults.tif catalogue only (Euclidean distance transform).

Signature: dots placed 300–900 m (3–9 px) from known faults — the step-over/splay zone where new geothermal splays are likely but unmapped.

Why it catches uncatalogued faults: Known faults are already mapped; new faults near-but-not-on them share the geothermal system without being re-detections.

Differs from repo: Not ML-driven; purely geometric annulus targeting. Contrasts with H19-5 family (>2 px from catalogue, ML field) and SWGB (>600 m, HGB field).

Expected DTI gain: Unknown — no holdout evaluated. Cost: low (single script, no training).

Verdict: Generated and gate-validated; holdout not evaluated.

H2 — Thermal-anchor emission prior (not yet generated)

Layers: GDR 1391 warm/hot springs, geothermometer wells, paleo sinter/tufa, 2 m heat-flow probes (label-free geothermal indicators).

Signature: distance-to-anchor + along-strike alignment; concentrate dots near geothermal surface expression.

Why it catches uncatalogued faults: Geothermal vents mark hidden fault conduits; anchors are independent of the fault catalogue.

Differs from repo: H-TVGB used anchors as learner features (measured negative, paired Δ −0.0124); this uses them as an emission prior only.

Expected DTI gain: Unknown. Cost: medium (needs GDR CSV downloads; outside sandbox allowlist).

Data needed: GDR 1391 well/spring CSV, paleo deposits ZIP, 2 m probe ZIP — all free, official, at gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391.

H3 — Curvature/tilt lineament density (not yet generated)

Layers: 1 m / 2 m lidar DEM derivatives (profile curvature, plan curvature, tilt).

Signature: curvature anomaly density in a 500 m window; lineaments not coincident with mapped faults.

Why it catches uncatalogued faults: Small scarps and fault-related folds create curvature signatures below mapping threshold.

Differs from repo: Repo uses scarp morphology (ex_max, lapneg_max, etc.) but not curvature-tilt density as a primary emission driver.

Expected DTI gain: Unknown. Cost: medium (needs lidar derivative stack).

H4 — Radiometric alteration halo edge (not yet generated)

Layers: GeoDAWN radiometric K, Th, U, TMI; hydrothermal alteration minerals (kaolinite, illite).

Signature: gradient edge of K-depletion / Th-enrichment halos around concealed faults.

Why it catches uncatalogued faults: Concealed faults host alteration halos invisible in lidar but detectable in radiometrics.

Differs from repo: Repo uses TMI gradient and radiometric bands as learner features, not as halo-edge emission drivers.

Expected DTI gain: Unknown. Cost: medium (GeoDAWN stack already mirrored).

H5 — Seismicity-dilation strain (not yet generated)

Layers: USGS earthquake catalog (M≥1.5, last 5 years) + InSAR line-of-sight velocity.

Signature: strain accumulation from seismic moment dilation; dots near aseismic creep zones.

Why it catches uncatalogued faults: Active aseismic faults may not appear on USGS/INGENIOUS maps but show in seismicity/InSAR.

Differs from repo: No seismicity or InSAR layer exists in the repo feature stack.

Expected DTI gain: Unknown. Cost: high (needs USGS earthquake API + InSAR data; outside sandbox allowlist for USGS).

Named non-fault mimics (all hypotheses): pluvial Lake Lahontan shorelines, fluvial terrace risers, road/canal embankments, basaltic vent rims, lithologic contacts — linear features parallel to mapped faults without being faults.
