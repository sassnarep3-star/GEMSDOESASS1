What remains a hypothesis

Removing predictions with little credit for hidden new faults could reduce weighted false-positive mass while retaining recall. But nearness to an existing fault does not make a dot automatically false: a new nearby splay could earn credit. The upstream prose overstates that point.

Without hidden truth or a submission-page receipt, the reported 0.2778 is not a verified causal result, and it does not calibrate a forecast for a new method.

Can we exceed the reported 0.2778? It is possible in principle, but this experiment supplies no evidence that it can. Conflicting user-provided highs of 0.3195 and 0.4413 are not treated as the current leaderboard. No live official ranking was retrieved.

Prevent the [0,1] rejection

The shared writer rejects NaN, infinity, sentinels, negative values and values above one. It requires one float32 band, the reference grid, zero outside the footprint and zero support on known faults. It reopens and validates the TIFF and a one-TIFF ZIP before exposing outputs.

It never silently clips or fills invalid predictions. The original portal error's cause is unknown; NaN, a nodata sentinel or out-of-range values are possibilities, not diagnosed facts.
