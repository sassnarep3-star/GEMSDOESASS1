Why 0.2778 is the group's best — in one paragraph

The 0.2778 raster is, bit-for-bit, the 0.2600 dotted H19-5 raster with the 6,436 dots within 200 m of the known catalogue deleted (44,090 → 37,654). The metric taxes every emitted pixel at 0.2 and ignores known-fault pixels, so dots that re-detect a mapped scarp 1–2 px off its trace are pure cost unless a new fault happens to lie within 300 m. The ladder 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778 is that tax being removed. A catalogue-flank file (h66, dots 3–15 px from the catalogue) scored 0.0742, whereas the champion's dots are in the median 2 km from any mapped fault: live "new faults" are far-field. Beating 0.2778 therefore needs a better field, not a better prune. Full derivation: knowledge/01.
