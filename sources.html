Independent read-back

A literal gate conflict, proven from pixels

The registered r13 lattice covers 5,160,724 of 5,167,373 pixels in the pinned owner-mirror footprint within three pixels, leaving 6,649 uncovered. For any K distinct in-footprint dots, at least max(0, K − 6,649) must be inside that halo.

At the common budget of 37,654 dots, at least 82.34% must overlap; the rule stops at more than 70%. At or above 22,164 dots the lower bound necessarily breaches the threshold. A smaller output is not automatically unique. This is a geometric preflight, not a score or a license to target uncovered holes.

We independently opened GEMSDOE36 KPL36: 37,341 / 37,654 = 99.17% overlap with the lattice, so the file fails even though its site has a “submit” banner. The file’s grid and in-footprint [0,1] checks pass locally; its surface and holdout were not independently replayed.

Known limitations

What the audit cannot certify

  • The owner-reported historical results do not have submission-page receipts that bind a score to a file hash.
  • The nine historical rasters are only a subset of a changing, broader registry; the complete current registry and pre-placement surfaces were not available.
  • The hash-pinned owner sample contains 60,988 ones identical to the label mirror, unlike the advertised absence sample. It was used only for grid and footprint; its organizer provenance is not verified.
  • Some historical mirrors have zero outside the irregular footprint; one has NaN nodata metadata. Official outside-bounds wording, the previous rejected file and portal acceptance need verification using the authorized sample.
  • No lane was assigned; no new feature, candidate, canary or holdout was produced. Two shared-template lineages are documented in the related sites.
Reproducibility

Re-run the audit locally

Python, gh and the dependencies in requirements.txt are needed. Fetching retrieves small hash-pinned owner witnesses to a Git-ignored directory; they are not candidate submissions or organizer-authenticated training data.

python -m venv .venv
. .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
python scripts/fetch_witnesses.py
python scripts/audit_witnesses.py --output evidence/witness-audit.json
python -m pytest -q

The independent audit does not replace the shared holdout evaluator, model, writer or selector. It never uploads a file.
