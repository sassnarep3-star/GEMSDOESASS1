## 4. Irregularities flagged for review

1. **Shipped file does not reproduce the protocol score.** Diagnostic holdout of the shipped (full-catalogue-pruned) dots is 0.0015. This is structural: the 2 px prune against the full catalogue deletes dots near the withheld faults. The family file h27-4 shows the same pattern (0.2708 live, 0.0314 holdout). The shipped file therefore needs its own design before any claim.
2. **The E2 winner is selected on the holdout.** 0.1674 is optimistic; a clean estimate needs a held-out sweep.
3. **Field construction.** The shipped field averages five fold models, each trained with 40 % of catalogue segments hidden. A single full-catalogue model is the proper live field and was not built.
4. **E1 crash and re-score.** Training completed and fields were saved; the result dict crashed on a duplicate keyword; scoring was redone from float16 fields (`run_tsfx.py e1score`). Float16 can change Poisson-disk tie-breaks; disclosed.
5. **Registry exclusions.** 412 of 414 rasters are comparable. Excluded: the calibration lattice (AMEND-LATTICE-01) and `GEMSDOE36/docs/downloads/GEMS36_FORMAT_TEST_NOT_SUBMISSION.tif` (32×48, incompatible grid). `GEMSDOE31/…/gemsdoe31-csdu-stepover-8cf9dc27-nan.tif` has 2.6 M nonfinite footprint values and was re-measured under AMEND-N2.
6. **Lattice policy.** AMEND-LATTICE-01 exempts only the 5 px calibration grid. The 0.2778 champion itself scores 99.9 % against it, so without the amendment every submission is a duplicate.
7. **Owner-reported leaderboard numbers** (0.4413, 0.3195, sibling scores) are unverified. No DrivenData login, no submission receipt, no weekly cap.
8. **Environment.** Sandbox RAM is 3 GB. The first canary attempt stalled the shell; it finished on re-check and its receipt was verified before use.
9. **Shared tool fixes (reported once, per protocol):** `scripts/fetch_reference_assets.sh` (sparse-checkout patterns passed as one string; single-file patterns needed a leading slash; checksum path relative to `data/`); `scikit-image` added to `requirements.txt` and lock (imported by `src/gems/holdout.py`, previously missing).

## 5. What is needed to produce a unique, submittable TIFF

- A **different emission geometry** from the H19-5 family (the 70 % rule is tripped by shared dot positions, not by the field's values alone). Options: score-density-matched dots that avoid 3 px of every registry dot, or a diffuse rather than dotted raster. Either must be pre-registered as a new lane, not tuned to pass the gate.
- A **full-catalogue single model** (not a fold average) and a **held-out emission sweep**.
- Official data and rules (DrivenData login) to confirm the scoring formula and the 0.4413 leaderboard context. Free official sources to check: USGS GeoDAWN (doi:10.5066/P93LGLVQ), INGENIOUS (gbcge.org), GDR submission 1391.
