## Independently reproduced byte findings

Run `python scripts/fetch_witnesses.py` and `python scripts/audit_witnesses.py --output evidence/witness-audit.json` in the audit environment. This audit performed no training, competition submission or holdout; a concurrent prior branch generated a separate synthetic research artifact. The script checks SHA-256, reads the actual pixels and grid, compares each of nine historical dot masks with all eight others, and reports the measurements. For these pinned binary witnesses, exact binary Spearman is calculated with ties over the common footprint; it is **not** a pre-placement surface check.

* All 72 directed historical comparisons exceed the literal 70% three-pixel-overlap limit. This is a scoped historic nine-file audit, not the complete current registry. The minimum is 86.71895% (GEMSDOE27 → GEMSDOE32). Every historical witness is therefore a duplicate relative to another checked witness, irrespective of filename or SHA.
* GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 historical masks are identical in the mirrored footprint. GEMSDOE32's B2 mask is exactly the GEMSDOE25 mask after removing pixels at Euclidean distance ≤2 pixels from the visible-label mirror. GEMSDOE32 is a subset of the GEMSDOE31 solo mask (2,545 removed pixels) and of the GEMSDOE36 historical mask (1,200 added pixels). The bytes do not establish what was uploaded, nor causality for user-reported scores.
* The pinned owner-mirror sample has 60,988 ones, exactly matching the owner-mirror labels. This conflicts with the public problem page's description of an absence-predicting sample. It is usable here **only for grid and footprint**, not as predictions or an authenticated sample. The sample may be mislabeled; no inference about organizer data is justified without a direct comparison.
* GEMSDOE36's KPL36 page says submit, but this exact KPL36 TIFF has 37,341 / 37,654 dots within 3 pixels of the pinned registered r13 lattice (99.1687%), breaching the literal >70% rule. Its grid and numeric range pass local read-back; its pre-placement surface and holdout were not independently replayed. Format is not a scientific or uniqueness pass. The later site material also says no file is approved; the page is internally inconsistent.
* Concurrent [PR #3](https://github.com/sassnarep3-star/GEMSDOESASS1/pull/3) introduced synthetic research TIFFs, preserved in this repository. Independent read-back found **37,596 / 37,654 = 99.8460%** of their dots within 3 pixels of `reg-052` (duplicate; DO NOT SUBMIT). Their in-footprint masks match exactly; the zero variant is finite and [0,1] throughout, while the NaN twin has NaN outside. The prior claim that the file was unique considered a narrower comparison and does not override this stop. Its reported holdout and prediction-to-score projection were not independently reproduced. The shared evaluator, original generation code and pre-placement surface are absent. The ZIP contains the identical zero-variant bytes.
* Against the nine pinned historical witnesses alone, our maximum directed overlap for the parallel artifact is **16.63%**, and versus GEMSDOE32 B2 it is **15.14%**. The earlier research audit claimed 20.62% versus B2; its exact scope or metric is unavailable, so the discrepancy is not silently reconciled. Passing either narrow comparison would not make it registry-unique.
* The pinned lattice has 5,160,724 covered and 6,649 uncovered pixels in the 5,167,373-pixel owner footprint. For K distinct candidate dots inside that footprint, at least `max(0, K - 6649)` lie in the lattice's halo. At K=37,654 this is at least 82.3418%, making strict uniqueness **mathematically impossible at that budget**. The first forced failure is K=22,164. For smaller K the bound may be inconclusive, not a pass. No other registry was exhaustively checked.
* GEMSDOE31's audited `allfinite` historical mirror has zeros outside the owner footprint yet NaN nodata metadata. All historical mirrors and KPL36 are zeros outside this mirror footprint. The official page says null/NaN outside *bounds*; a zeros-only file may behave differently from the authorized sample. The previous portal rejection lacks preserved bytes or a staff diagnosis, so its cause and portal acceptance cannot be asserted.
* Different sites report multiple template/evaluator lineages and contradictory approval banners. Holdout values from different evaluators are not directly rankable; an owner claim is not an organizer-confirmed score. This checkout contains no prepared shared stack, candidate lane, frozen same-protocol best, or complete registry.

## Interpreting the best reported result

The owner report associates GEMSDOE32 B2 with **0.2778 — USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED**; it is the highest among the nine *supplied historical claims*. The official metric gives each truth pixel its best nearby weighted prediction while false-positive mass still costs; spreading high-confidence points roughly at kernel scale and removing low-credit redundant mass *could* help. But additional pruning can also lose true fault coverage, especially since the organizer says new faults can be close to known ones. No hidden TP/FP/FN, original upload or receipt was available, so this is a **hypothesis**, not a causal proof or a calibrated forecast. We cannot establish that any new file beats the reported value, or the current public leader.
