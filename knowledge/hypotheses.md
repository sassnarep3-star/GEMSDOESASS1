## Frozen BGAC-1 experiment

- One hypothesis experiment; two fixed physical scales are a single formula, not a search. Controls and per-feature ablations are diagnostics, not alternatives to select for submission.
- Reuse `vendor/gems57/prepare_data.py` feature cache, `spatial.folds`, `evaluate_holdout.py`, and `submission_writer.py` from pinned GEMSDOE32. No private scoring implementation.
- Whole original 8-connected catalogue components intersecting each fixed spatial quadrant are hidden in full, including cross-quadrant tails. Training exclusion is the quadrant plus hidden component tails with an 80-pixel buffer. No fitting is performed in this label-free experiment. Features never read the catalogue.
- Pixel-exact visible-fault masking. Pooled DTI with alpha=0.2, beta=0.8, triangular radius 300 m; physical 20 km block bootstrap, 1,999 resamples, 95% percentile intervals. These are conditional **HOLDOUT-DTI**, not leaderboard estimates.
- Each feature alone receives a holdout AUC canary and a DTI diagnostic. AUC >0.90 (or <0.10, an inverse leak) vetoes trust pending investigation. Catalogue-zero pixels are unlabeled background, not verified negatives.
- Fixed 0.73% dot budget of eligible pixels per fold, 3-pixel Euclidean exclusion, deterministic stable priority. No tuning after results.
- Compare with the strongest contemporaneous label-free ablation on the same folds and budget. Do NOT compare directly to historical holdout values with different splits. Prior frozen rasters are for novelty/history only: they were built with the full catalogue and cannot be fair training controls.
- Exact full-footprint Spearman correlation against all matching single-band TIFFs in the nine-site public registry before placement. Literal candidate-directed >70% positive-support overlap within <=3 pixels after placement, with no density exemptions. Rank correlation >0.90 OR overlap >0.70 => duplicate, stop, no submission artifact.
- A format-valid candidate may be packaged only after duplicate and canary clearance. Scientific release additionally requires paired improvement CI lower bound >0, beating a compatible current holdout best, independent data/rule verification, and complete required registry scope. A separate selector approves any weekly slot. This run never uploads.
- Hard stop after three experiments or two hours. This run plans one. Failures and negative results remain deliverables.
